02 / About

Connecting practice
with evidence.

I am a higher education professional working across instructional design, educational technology, learning analytics and applied data science.

My work is grounded in a practical question: how can institutions use technology and evidence to improve the learning experience, support teaching practice and make better decisions?

I bring experience from school education and university teaching and support, alongside postgraduate study in educational technology, open distance learning and data analytics. I am particularly interested in the realities of teaching and learning in rural and resource-constrained higher education environments.

04 / Research

Questions worth
investigating.

My research interests sit at the intersection of digital capability, teaching practice, student experience and institutional decision-making.

I am interested in research that is methodologically sound and useful in context, especially in rural and resource-constrained higher education.

01

Digital competencies in higher education

How lecturers and students understand, develop and apply digital competencies in their teaching and learning contexts.

02

Learning analytics and student success

How educational data can support responsible, interpretable and context-aware student support.

03

Technology-enhanced and blended learning

How instructional design and institutional conditions shape meaningful technology integration.

04

AI in teaching and learning

Practical, critical use of AI tools, including questions of accuracy, access, assessment and educator agency.
